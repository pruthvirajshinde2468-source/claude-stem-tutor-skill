Parts found in the photo

    Two loops. The Pi sends commands only (mA). The motor current (A) flows in a separate loop: supply + → VM → H-bridge → coil → Rsense → GND → supply −. It never goes through the Pi.

    Model simplifications. The motor shows a 2-pole rotor and 4 stator poles. A real 1.8° stepper has 8 stator poles with small teeth and a 50-tooth rotor, so it turns 50× less per step. The driver panel is a schematic, not the chip layout. The chopper PWM (about 20–40 kHz) is not shown. The glow speed is not the real electron speed (real drift is below 1 mm/s). One switch powers both the Pi and the motor supply.
