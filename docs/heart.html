Cardiac cycle (Wiggers diagram)

Pressure–volume loop (left ventricle)

CO = SV × HR. One loop = one beat. The width of the loop is the stroke volume.

Sources. Timing of the cycle (0.8 s: atrial systole 0.1 s, ventricular systole 0.3 s, joint diastole 0.4 s), SV ≈ 70 mL, CO ≈ 5 L/min: NCERT Biology Class XI, ch. "Body Fluids and Circulation". Pressures and volumes: Guyton & Hall, Textbook of Medical Physiology (cardiac cycle chapter). Model: time-varying elastance (Suga & Sagawa 1974), closed-loop lumped circulation.
Model simplifications. Chambers are ellipsoids. Valves are ideal one-way doors. The ECG shape comes from the activation timing, not from cell electricity. The PR interval is shortened to fit the NCERT timing.
